Health Content Quality & Governance Standard

Mandatory checklist for every live article prior to publication
gavel Verified Safe for Community Guidance
task_alt Mandatory Article Components
  • check Common Symptoms: Objective, layperson descriptions of conditions.
  • check Possible Causes: Clear educational non-definitive explanations without diagnosing.
  • check When to See a Doctor: Explicit red flags, worsening criteria, and timeline warnings.
  • check Specialty Match: Linked prompt guiding user to local specialty search (e.g. ENT, Cardiology).
block Strict Editorial Prohibitions
  • close No Diagnosis: Must not claim to diagnose any specific patient condition.
  • close No Prescriptions: Absolute prohibition of pharmaceutical dosages or medicines.
  • close No Doctor Endorsements: Never claim any individual practitioner is superior.
  • close Zero Commercial Bias: Strict ban on sponsored clinic promotions or paid endorsements.
