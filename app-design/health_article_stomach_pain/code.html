Stomach Pain

Stomach pain can happen for many different reasons. Some causes are temporary, while others may need medical attention.

help

What is it?

Stomach pain or discomfort refers to aching, cramps, or a dull heavy feeling anywhere between the chest and lower abdomen. Most everyday stomach discomfort is mild and improves with rest, drinking clean water, and simple food.

format_list_bulleted

Common symptoms

People often experience stomach pain alongside other signs, such as:

check
Pain or cramping in the stomach area
check
Bloating or a tight, full feeling
check
Nausea, feeling sick, or reduced appetite
check
Changes in digestion, loose motion, or difficulty passing stool
check
Mild acidity, gas, or sour burps
psychology_alt

Common causes

Stomach discomfort can have many possible everyday causes. Common reasons include:

• Eating irregular meals, very oily or spicy food
• Indigestion or acidity after heavy food
• Drinking contaminated water or mild stomach infection
• Gas buildup and constipation
• Stress, fatigue, or travel changes
info

Because symptoms can look similar, only a qualified doctor can determine the exact cause.

medical_services

When should you see a doctor?

Consider speaking with a doctor if:

arrow_right The pain lasts more than 2 to 3 days or keeps coming back
arrow_right You have trouble eating, drinking fluids, or sleeping
arrow_right Vomiting or loose motion does not stop after 24 hours
arrow_right The discomfort is interfering with your daily work or family duties
warning

When to seek urgent medical help

Seek urgent medical help at the nearest hospital or health center if you notice:

! Severe, sudden, or unbearable pain
! Pain spreading to chest, neck, or shoulder
! High fever with chills or persistent vomiting
! Blood in vomit or dark, black stool
! Fainting, severe dizziness, or difficulty breathing
